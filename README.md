# DrasticMagicDuels
A 2D videogame of magic duels made in Unity by Giovanni Battilana, music by Fabio Cuccu.

## Features
- The game has 2 wizards who challenge each other in a magic duel
- The left wizard is controlled by the user, the right one is controlled by CPU
- The wizards can cast offensive spell, the Strikando, to each other
- The wizards can also cast Defendo, the defensive spell, which blocks and reflects Strikando
- The wizards can also dodge opponent offensive spell by dashing

## Controls
WASD to move your player
Space to dash while moving
Left mouse click to cast Strikando at the viewfinder
Hold left mouse click for one second to cast a more powerwful version of Strikando
Right mouse click to cast Defendo

## Technologies
- Unity 6
- C#
